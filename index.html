About the owner

Hello, I am Rahul Sharma

I started Sharma Furniture in 2019 after years of learning the trade beside my uncle in his small carpentry workshop. I still remember sanding my first wardrobe door by hand and checking it three times before anyone saw it.

Over seven years I have noticed one thing: most homes in Chikalsandra do not have standard rooms. Walls are uneven and corners are tight. So we measure properly, plan the space with you and build to fit.

When you order from us you deal with me and my team directly, from the first measurement to the last screw. If something is not right, we fix it.

Rahul Sharma, Owner
